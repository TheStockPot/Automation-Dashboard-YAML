# Everyday Automations

These are the automations from the "automations we use literally every day" video — most don't rely on expensive smart devices or complex integrations, so chances are you've already got what you need to set a few of these up yourself.

All the YAML and setup instructions are below. Each link goes to a README with the required helpers, entities, and full automation YAML, ready to drop into Home Assistant and adapt with your own entity IDs.

- **[Baby Lamp](Automations/Baby%20Lamp.md)** — Brings a nursery lamp on at a low brightness when the door opens, boosts it for a nappy change, and turns it off again once you leave.
- **[Bedtime & Wakeup](Automations/Bedtime%20%26%20Wakeup.md)** — Handles bedside lamps, AC zoning, and blinds automatically every night and morning.
- **[Washing Machine & Dryer Notifications](Automations/Washing%20Machine%20Notifications.md)** — Notifies whoever's home when a cycle finishes, and waits until someone's actually home if nobody's around to act on it.
- **[Festoon Lights](Automations/Festoon%20Lights.md)** — Turns pergola lighting on automatically when it's dark and you head outside, and off again when you close up or head to bed.
- **[Rain Notification](Automations/Rain%20Notification.md)** — Lets you know the moment it starts raining, so you can get the washing off the line before it's soaked.
- **[Britt Leaving Work](Automations/Britt%20Leaving%20Work.md)** — Sends an ETA when Britt leaves work, then arms the kettle to boil as she's nearly home.
- **[Home & Away](Automations/Home%20%26%20Away.md)** — Prepares the house for being empty when everyone leaves, and restores it when someone's on their way back.
